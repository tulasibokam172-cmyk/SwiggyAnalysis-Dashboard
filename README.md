📊 Swiggy Food Delivery Analysis – Excel Dashboard
This project presents a 3-layer analytics dashboard built in Microsoft Excel, providing insights into customer behaviour, restaurant performance, and delivery efficiency for a Swiggy-like food delivery system.

The dashboard transforms raw data into clear, actionable business intelligence using pivot tables, charts, slicers, and Excel formulas.

📁 Project Overview
The goal of this project is to analyze end-to-end food delivery operations and answer key business questions such as:

Which food categories and restaurants perform best?

How do customer demographics influence order behaviour?

Which cities generate higher revenue?

How does delivery distance impact delivery time?

What are the reasons for low customer ratings?

The solution is structured into three dashboards for easy interpretation.

📊 Key Business Insights
Age group 26–35 places the highest number of orders.

Biryani is the highest-revenue food category.

Pune & Mumbai generate strong revenue and repeat customers.

Delivery time increases with distance, causing lower ratings.

UPI & Cash dominate payment methods.

Restaurants with consistently low ratings require operational improvements.
